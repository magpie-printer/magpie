# Z Tensioner Pulley Cover

My Z tensioner pulley started to lift up out of its track when the belt was tensioned, so I designed this cover.  It screws into the stock bottom middle frame piece and the tensioner extension.

### Hardware
- 2x M3x40 (Slightly longer bolts for mounting to `Front_frame_tensioner`.)
- 1x M3x8 for mounting to the `Tensioner_extension`

### Installation
To install, drill a hole in the top of the `Tensioner_extension` small enough that it can be self tapped by an M3 bolt.  Screw the piece into `Front_frame_tensioner` with the M3x40 bolts and screw the other side in with the M3x8 bolt.
